## What would flip a no

- Seeing real unboxing videos from Baghdad girls or a friend I trust ordering from them and getting her item right.
- A wholesale rate for bulk orders from 1688: a lower price per kilo for big shipments and a lower buying fee, with a guaranteed delivery date.
- Full cash on delivery, I pay nothing until it arrives, or friends I know who already got their orders from them.
- If a mother I know ordered from them and it came on time, and the total for kids' clothes was clearly cheaper than my page, plus they found the item for me so I don't have to search Taobao.
- If a relative or neighbour ordered from them a few times without problems and the total fees came out clearly cheaper than my current service.
- A lower per-kilo rate for bulk reseller orders, plus a few real unboxing videos from people I follow showing the stuff arrived as ordered.
- Letting me pay everything on delivery for my first order, or a trader I know in Shorja vouching that they've used them for months without problems.
- A lower per-kilo rate for shop owners who order several kilos a month, and a trial order where I see the warehouse photos and the parcel arrives on time.
- If they showed me a real quote that beats my usual page by at least 5,000 dinars on the same item, and a friend I trust ordered from them and got it.
- A bulk or wholesale rate, something like under 8,000 IQD per kilo with a lower buying fee on big orders, plus a guaranteed delivery date.
- Seeing real customers from Baghdad posting their unboxings and the warehouse photos on Instagram, with a friend or someone I follow saying they got their refund when something was wrong.
- Seeing women I know, or a page with real customer videos and comments, get their orders delivered from them, or letting me pay everything on delivery for my first order.
