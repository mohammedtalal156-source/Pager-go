# بغداد إكسبريس | Baghdad Express

- What it is: An Iraqi shopping-agent and cross-border shipping service. The customer sends a product link (or a sourcing request), pays in IQD, and Baghdad Express buys from the foreign store, receives the goods at a partner warehouse in China, inspects, consolidates, ships to Iraq, clears customs and delivers.
- Who it is for: (1) Individuals in Iraq (Baghdad first) buying clothes, electronics, kids' items, home goods and parts from Taobao, 1688, Pinduoduo, AliExpress, JD; later Trendyol and Amazon/eBay. (2) Iraqi shop owners and small importers who need wholesale buying, supplier sourcing, inspection and consolidation from China.
- What it sells, at what price: A purchasing-agent service plus international shipping. Price = product cost + China domestic shipping + agent commission (working assumption 8–12% with a minimum for individuals, 3–6% for merchants) + international shipping per chargeable kg (partner cost + 15–25% margin) + clearance + Iraq delivery. All margins are planning assumptions, not validated.
- Where and how: Online (PWA in Arabic, plus WhatsApp/Instagram at launch). Delivery in Baghdad first, other governorates later. Back-end operations through a white-label logistics partner in Guangzhou/Yiwu (not yet chosen; candidates: Kreenex, Sels Express, AKQ Logistics, ZMC Express, SMT Shipment, 611 Cargo — none verified or contracted).
- Budget and constraints: Budget not yet stated. No registered company, import licence, payment licence, partner contract or integrations exist yet. Payments must go through licensed Iraqi providers. Launch market: China → Iraq; Turkey and USA later.
